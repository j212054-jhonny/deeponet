## 1. Starting point: the UNISIM-I-D PINN (Model 1)

Everything in this folder **originates from, and reuses without modification,** the work already
done for the PINN on the UNISIM-I-D benchmark (`SCMIL UNISIM1/MODELO 1/`, master
`MASTER_UNISIM1_MODEL1_PINN_COMPLETO_15_09_2026.md`). The PINN solves **a single physical instance**
(one fixed `K` field, one fixed well control) with a network that approximates the solution of the
Buckley-Leverett PDE. Four elements are inherited from it, unchanged:

| Element inherited from the PINN | What it is | Why it is not re-derived |
|---|---|---|
| `K` field (permeability) | The same deterministic field from `UNISIM_I_D_WF.DATA` (not the probabilistic ensemble — only the *deterministic* version of UNISIM-I-D was downloaded) | Varying `K` requires new geostatistics; out of scope under the time constraint (preparing a class) |
| Cutout geometry | 2D, layer `k=2`, wells `INJ1` (injector) + `NA2` (producer) | Same cutout already validated by the PINN (master §2); reusing it avoids re-justifying the 3D→2D simplification |
| Grid, PVT, `SWOF` | Identical to the baseline | Same reasoning -- already validated and not the subject of this chapter |
| BHP limits (`480 bar` max for INJ1, `230 bar` min for NA2) | Fixed, do not vary in any scenario | They preserve the strict two-phase (water/oil, no free gas) regime that the project deliberately tuned already -- reopening this would reopen an already-solved physics problem |
| Baseline breakthrough time (`RATE 4000`/`LRAT 3000`) | `~1.7 years` for the water front to travel the ~1170 m between `INJ1` and `NA2` in layer `k=2` | Measured directly on `unisim1_model1.h5` (a file already generated by the PINN), with no re-simulation -- see §3 |

**What DOES change relative to the PINN:** the PINN solves one fixed physics; a neural operator
(DeepONet or CPNO) learns the **solution operator** -- a function that maps *any* combination of well
controls `(RATE_INJ1, LRAT_NA2)` to the corresponding `Sw(x,y,t)` and `P(x,y,t)` fields, without
needing to solve the PDE again for every new case (Lu et al. 2019). That requires **many**
input-output pairs -- which is where everything else in this folder comes from: the 50 training/
validation scenarios and the 10 independent test scenarios.

## 2. Why 50 scenarios (and not 1, as with the PINN)

**50 is consistent with the literature, not an arbitrary number:**

- Cui et al. (2025) show that 50 equivalent high-fidelity runs + physics constraints in the loss cut
  error by ~90% compared to training with pure data alone.
- The Egg Model (the community's standard benchmark, 101 realizations, 8 injectors + 4 producers) is
  trained successfully using MLPs with only **10** realizations (Ghahfarokhi 2022, R²>0.98).
- Badawi et al. (2024) and Huang et al. (2023) use precisely **varying well controls** (not the `K`
  field) as the surrogate's input -- the same variation axis chosen here.
- No precedent was found of a DeepONet trained specifically on UNISIM-I-D -- as far as could be
  verified, this chapter is the first documented one.

See §7 (Literature) for the full references with links.

## 3. Measured breakthrough -- "established fact", not an estimate

Measured directly on `unisim1_model1.h5` (the file already generated by the PINN, with no
re-simulation), the `Sw` saturation at the `NA2` cell within layer `k=2` specifically (not the
aggregated well-level `WWPR`, which mixes other completion layers and gives a false water signal
from the first month):

| Year | `Sw` at NA2 (layer k=2) |
|---|---|
| 0.00 – 1.67 | stable at `0.1700 -> 0.1704` (no real movement -- numerical noise) |
| **1.75** | **`0.2401`** <- the front arrives here |
| 1.92 | `0.4061` |
| 2.50 | `0.5356` |
| 10.0 | `0.7054` |

**The front takes ~1.7 years** with the base rates (`RATE 4000`/`LRAT 3000`). Notable coincidence:
`1.7 years / T* (34.4 years) ≈ 0.049`, practically identical to `CURRICULUM_T_FLOOR=0.05`, which the
PINN team found through hyperparameter sweeps, unrelated to this calculation -- an independent
cross-validation that `t̂≈0.05` is not arbitrary.

**Horizon chosen for the new scenarios: 5 years** (~3x margin over the base breakthrough, needed
because varying `RATE`/`LRAT` can delay breakthrough).

## 4. The 50 training/validation scenarios

### 4.1 Parameters and sampling

| Parameter | Range | Distribution |
|---|---|---|
| `RATE_INJ1` | `[2000, 6000] sm3/d` (0.5x-1.5x of the 4000 baseline) | 2D Latin Hypercube Sampling, 50 points, `SEED=42` |
| `LRAT_NA2` | `[1500, 4500] sm3/d` (0.5x-1.5x of the 3000 baseline) | same LHS, coupled |

LHS was chosen (not a regular grid or pure random sampling) for better coverage of the 2D space with
the same number of runs (Salehian et al. 2022). Note, not a bug: some combinations make a well hit
its BHP limit before reaching its target rate -- OPM automatically switches from rate control to BHP
control, giving examples of different control regimes.

### 4.2 Generation pipeline (real, executed 10/04-05/2026)

```
[Local, WSL -- flow already installed]
 1. Generate 50 decks from the UNISIM_I_D_WF.DATA template
    (RATE_INJ1 / LRAT_NA2 per the LHS table; SCHEDULE trimmed to 5 years / 60 steps)
 2. flow --output-dir=runs/escenario_NN escenario_NN.DATA   x 50
    -> ~6.1 GB raw (full 3D, 20 layers) -- TEMPORARY, discarded afterwards
 3. Extraction (resdata + h5py): layer k=2 only, pressure + swat
    -> unisim1_deeponet_dataset.h5 (114.8 MB) -- the ONLY persisted artifact
 4. Manual upload to Google Drive (MyDrive/SCMIL_UNISIM1_OPERADOR_NEURAL/datasets/)

[Colab -- for training only, as many times as needed]
 5. Mount Drive, read unisim1_deeponet_dataset.h5
 6. Train DeepONet/CPNO -- OPM is never invoked again
```


### 4.4 Extending the training set beyond 50

The 5 interpolation scenarios (I1-I5, §5) are already real OPM runs with the same `K`/grid/PVT as the
50 -- folding them into training would be pure array concatenation, with no re-simulation needed.
**This is not done today** because I1-I5 (together with E1-E5) are precisely the set reserved to
measure honest generalization; moving them into training would remove that function. If the 50 ever
prove insufficient, the correct route is to generate **new** scenarios (new `RATE_INJ1`/`LRAT_NA2`
points, not the 10 already reserved) with the same pipeline (`scripts/run_batch_50.sh` +
`scripts/extraer_50_escenarios.py`, adapting the LHS range/seed) and concatenate their extraction
into the training `.h5` -- the same concatenation pattern already used by
`SCMIL_OPM_OPERADOR_NEURAL_Exploracion_60_Escenarios_EN.ipynb` (Section 1) to combine train+test.
This does require running OPM again (~1.65 min/new scenario), unlike I1-I5 which already exist.

## 5. The 10 independent test scenarios (ESC50-ESC59)

An extension added after the 50 (not part of the original DeepONet plan), generated to measure
generalization outside the set used for training/k-fold, with **the same** `K`, PVT, grid, wells,
5-year horizon, 61 snapshots and BHP limits as the 50 base scenarios -- only
`(RATE_INJ1, LRAT_NA2)` changes.

### 5.1 Composition: 5 interpolation + 5 stress

| Scenario | Tag | Group | `RATE_INJ1` (sm3/d) | `LRAT_NA2` (sm3/d) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50 | I1 | interpolation | 2400.00 | 1800.00 |
| 51 | I2 | interpolation | 3200.00 | 4200.00 |
| 52 | I3 | interpolation | 3920.00 | 3000.00 |
| 53 | I4 | interpolation | 4800.00 | 2400.00 |
| 54 | I5 | interpolation | 5600.00 | 3600.00 |
| 55 | E1 | stress (rate) | 6200.00 | 1800.00 |
| 56 | E2 | stress (rate) | 6200.00 | 3000.00 |
| 57 | E3 | stress (lrat) | 2400.00 | 4650.00 |
| 58 | E4 | stress (lrat) | 4800.00 | 4650.00 |
| 59 | E5 | stress (combined) | 6200.00 | 4650.00 |

- **Interpolation (I1-I5):** points **inside** the training range (`RATE_INJ1 ∈ [2000,6000]`,
  `LRAT_NA2 ∈ [1500,4500]`) but not coincident with any of the 50 LHS points -- these measure how well
  the operator interpolates within the already-sampled region.
- **Stress (E1-E5):** points that push toward or **slightly exceed** the training range
  (`RATE_INJ1` up to 6200, `LRAT_NA2` up to 4650, both ~3% above the training maximum) -- these
  measure extrapolation near the edge, which is exactly the question already investigated in
  Sections 8/13 of the DeepONet and CPNO notebooks (error per scenario vs. position in LHS space).

### 5.2 Generation and verification pipeline (same pattern as the 50)

Dedicated scripts, isolated from the training ones so the sets are never mixed:

- `scripts/run_batch_10_test.sh` -- generates/runs the 10 decks (`ESC50`-`ESC59`) into `runs_test/`.
- `scripts/extraer_10_test.py` -- extracts layer `k=2` (`pressure`+`swat`) from `runs_test/` and
  `decks/params_lhs_10_test.csv`, consolidating a **separate** `.h5`:
  `unisim1_deeponet_test10.h5` -- never mixed with `unisim1_deeponet_dataset.h5` (the 50).
- Physical verification built into the script itself: `0.0 <= Sw <= 1.0` on every active cell of the
  10 scenarios before saving the `.h5` (explicit assert).
- Evaluation of the trained models (DeepONet V1b) against this test set:
  `SCMIL UNISIM1 DEEPONET/notebooks/DeepONet_V1b_Evaluacion_Test10.ipynb`.

### 5.3 Format of `unisim1_deeponet_test10.h5`

Same schema as the 50-scenario dataset, plus test metadata (`tag`, `grupo`, `escenario_id`) and well
curves (`WBHP`/`WWIR` at INJ1, `WBHP`/`WOPR`/`WWPR` at NA2), resampled onto `time_days` by
interpolation:

```text
unisim1_deeponet_test10.h5
├─ static/    # identical to the 50-scenario dataset (same K, same k=2)
├─ scenarios/
│  ├─ params         (10, 2)   # [RATE_INJ1, LRAT_NA2]
│  ├─ escenario_id    (10,)     # 50..59
│  ├─ tag             (10,)     # "I1".."E5"
│  ├─ grupo           (10,)     # "interpolacion" / "estres_rate" / "estres_lrat" / "estres_combinado"
│  ├─ time_days        (61,)
│  ├─ pressure         (10, 61, 81, 58)
│  ├─ swat             (10, 61, 81, 58)
│  └─ wells/
│     ├─ INJ1_wbhp, INJ1_wwir     (10, 61)
│     └─ NA2_wbhp, NA2_wopr, NA2_wwpr   (10, 61)
```

## 7. Supporting literature (Consensus, Oct 2026)

- Lu, L. et al. (2019). *Learning nonlinear operators via DeepONet...* Nature Machine Intelligence --
  branch/trunk architecture, motivates why a neural operator needs many input-output pairs.
- Cui, J.-W. et al. (2025). *Efficient Training of Deep-Learning-Based Surrogate Models for
  Subsurface Flow Simulation Using Multifidelity Data and Physics Constraints*. SPE Journal -- 50
  equivalent runs + physics constraints, -90% error vs. data-only.
- Jansen, J. et al. (2014). *The egg model -- a geological ensemble for reservoir simulation*.
  Geoscience Data Journal -- the community's standard benchmark (101 realizations).
- Ghahfarokhi, A.J. (2022). *Adaptive Proxy-based robust production optimization with multilayer
  perceptron*. Applied Computing and Geosciences -- MLP with only 10 realizations of the Egg Model.
- Badawi, D. et al. (2024). *Neural Operator-Based Proxy for Reservoir Simulations Considering
  Varying Well Settings, Locations, and Permeability Fields*. Computational Geosciences -- varying
  well controls as the surrogate's input.
- Huang, H. et al. (2023). *A Deep-Learning-Based Graph Neural Network-LSTM Model for Reservoir
  Simulation... With Varying Well Controls*. SPE Journal -- 500 runs with varying controls.
- Salehian, M. et al. (2022). *Multi-solution well placement optimization using ensemble learning of
  surrogate models*. J. Petroleum Science and Engineering -- adapted LHS for surrogate datasets.
- Santos et al. (2025). *Hybrid DeepONet Surrogates for Multiphase Flow in Porous Media*,
  arXiv:2511.02962 -- CNN branch + MLP trunk architecture used in `DeepONet_V1a/V1b`.
- Chen et al. (2026). *Physics-Informed Chebyshev Polynomial Neural Operator for Parametric PDEs*,
  arXiv:2602.01737 -- Algorithm 1 used in `CPNO_V1a`.
